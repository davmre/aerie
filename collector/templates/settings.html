Usage Notes

After saving your Bluesky credentials, you can poll your timeline using the command line: 

cd collector
python bluesky_poller.py --watch

The poller will fetch posts from your Bluesky home timeline and store them in the database for classification alongside your Twitter content.

Note: Settings saved here are stored in the local database. Environment variables (BLUESKY_HANDLE, BLUESKY_APP_PASSWORD) take precedence if set.
